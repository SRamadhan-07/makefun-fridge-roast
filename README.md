A one-line roast aimed squarely at the smart fridge itself. The fridge in the line has time to order the milk, judge your diet, and sell advertising space on its own door, yet its ice maker — the one job that actually matters — is broken, as ever. The joke targets the fridge, its priorities, and its ice maker, not the person who owns it.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the entry line exactly to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` when needed) and prints nothing else.
